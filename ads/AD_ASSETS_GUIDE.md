## 🎨 RECOMMENDED TOOLS FOR PRODUCTION ADS

### Option 1: Canva (Easiest)
1. Go to **canva.com/create/ads/**
2. Search "Carousel Ad" or "Social Media Post"
3. Choose 1080x1080 or 1080x1920
4. Drag & drop car images
5. Add text overlays
6. Export as JPG/PNG

### Option 2: Adobe Express (Free)
1. **adobe.com/express/create/ad**
2. Choose Instagram Post (1080x1080)
3. Search "car" in Elements
4. Add your car images
5. Export

### Option 3: CapCut (Mobile Video)
1. Download CapCut app
2. Import car videos/images
3. Add transitions & music
4. Export 9:16 (1080x1920)

- **Pexels.com** - Search "Mercedes", "luxury car"

### Car Image Tips
1. Use bright, clear photos
2. Show car from best angle
3. Include interior shots
4. Add 10% padding (Meta crops)

## 📤 EXPORT SETTINGS

### For Meta Ads
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Quality: High (80%+)
- Color: sRGB

### For Google Ads
- Format: JPG, PNG, or GIF
- Quality: High
- File size: <150KB recommended

### For Video
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Bitrate: 5-10 Mbps
