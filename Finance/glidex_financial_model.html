6. Executive Summary of Financial Outlook

EVALUATION BRIEFING

1. Asset-Light Capital Efficiency: Because GlideX operates as a two-sided digital mobility marketplace connecting existing private vehicle owners with renters, the company does not incur vehicle acquisition debt, vehicle leasing liabilities, or fleet depreciation costs. Capital is deployed exclusively into platform technology, trust verification, and user acquisition.

2. Fast Path to Unit Profitability: With an average trip gross booking value of KES 16,500 ($127) and an 18.0% take rate, each rental generates KES 2,970 in net revenue against an estimated blended customer acquisition cost of KES 650. GlideX recovers user acquisition investment within the first completed booking.

3. Scale & Operating Leverage: As active fleet volume expands from 120 vehicles in Year 1 to 1,350 in Year 3 and 6,800 in Year 5, technology hosting and core staff costs scale at sub-linear rates, expanding EBITDA margins from 13.2% in 2026 to over 40% at scale.

4. Cash Flow & Self-Sustaining Operations: Operating cash flow turns positive in Year 1 under baseline assumptions, generating cumulative cash reserves of KES 50.5M by Year 3 and KES 464.4M by Year 5, supporting regional expansion across East Africa without continuous dilutive funding rounds.
